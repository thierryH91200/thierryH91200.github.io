Frequently asked questions

Is my data stored online?

No. All data is saved locally on your Mac. Nothing is sent to a remote server.

Can I import my bank data?

Yes. CSV file import is supported (more formats coming). Make sure your file follows the expected structure: date, amount, description, etc.

Can I manage several accounts?

Yes. Create and track as many accounts as you like: checking, savings, business…

Are transactions categorized automatically?

Semi-automatic categorization is offered: the app recognizes frequent labels to suggest categories, which you can adjust manually.

Is there a Windows or Linux version?

No. PegaseUIData is built with SwiftUI for macOS only. A cross-platform version is not planned in the short term.

How can I report a bug or offer a translation?

Email us at thierry.hentic@gmail.com with your macOS version, the steps to reproduce the issue and, if possible, a screenshot. Any help with translations is very welcome!
